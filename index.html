    Why two databases

    The central registry knows which projects exist and who may read them. The corpus holds the documents. Postgres cannot join across databases, so ownership is proved by scoping to the project ids the registry vouched for — never by a join.

    The filter is not optional

    Every corpus read goes through corpusDbFor() or resolveProjectContext(). Reaching a corpus table through the central handle is the one mistake the design cannot catch for you.
